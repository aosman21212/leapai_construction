About this Module عن هذه الوحدة

LeapAI Construction ERP is a comprehensive, fully Arabic-English bilingual construction management system built natively for Odoo 19. Designed specifically for Saudi Arabia's construction industry, it covers every aspect of project delivery — from initial planning and site operations to HSE compliance, financial management, equipment tracking, and document control.

Compliant with Saudi Vision 2030 requirements, including Nitaqat workforce classifications, GOSI integration readiness, VAT 15% calculations, and the 6-region administrative structure of the Kingdom.
